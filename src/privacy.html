Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-04. The site collects nothing about you.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts, forms that submit data, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No wallet connection and no request for signatures or addresses.
  • No third-party fonts, embeds or CDNs. Every file loads from the same place as this page.

The playground

The simulation runs entirely in your browser tab. Values you type stay in the page's memory and are gone when you close or reload it. Nothing is stored or sent.

Hosting

The site is a static export meant for IPFS or any static host. Whoever operates the gateway or host you use may keep standard request logs, such as IP address and time, under their own policy. The maintainers do not receive those logs.

External links

Links to official x402 materials and other sites leave this site. Their privacy policies apply there.
